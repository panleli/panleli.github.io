I am an MSc student in the [School of Computing Science at Simon Fraser University](https://www.sfu.ca/computing.html), supervised by [Prof. SiQi Zhou](https://siqizhou.com/). I received my bachelor’s degree in Statistics from the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/).

My research interests lie in **uncertainty, safety, and reliability in robotics**. I am particularly interested in understanding when a robot’s perception or environment representation may be unreliable, how this uncertainty should influence its subsequent actions, and how robotic systems can be evaluated more rigorously beyond overall task success rates.

My current work explores how semantic environment representations can be used to evaluate and verify robot plans generated by language models.

## Research Opportunities and Collaboration

I am happy to connect with students and researchers interested in robotics, robot learning, semantic scene understanding, 3D environment representations, and the safety, reliability, and evaluation of robotic systems.

SFU Computing Science undergraduate students interested in pursuing a Special Research Project through CMPT 415 or CMPT 416 are especially welcome to reach out about potential projects in Prof. Zhou’s lab. Depending on project fit and availability, I may serve as a mentor under Prof. Zhou’s supervision.

If you are interested, please email me at [pla113@sfu.ca](mailto:pla113@sfu.ca) with a brief introduction and a description of your background and research interests.
